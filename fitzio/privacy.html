Fitzio Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Fitzio is made by PCI Solutions (“we”).

Fitzio helps you follow a diet and training plan from your coach: what you eat, the workouts you do, and your weekly check-ins. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Fitzio works without an account. Until you sign in, everything you enter stays on your phone, in the app’s own storage.
  • If you sign in, your plans and logs are also stored in our cloud database so they’re backed up and available on your other devices.
  • Your progress photos never leave your phone.
  • If you use AI plan reading, the photo or document you choose is sent to an AI service to be read, and isn’t kept afterwards.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and we don’t sell your information.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

The camera and photo library are only used when you add a progress photo or choose a plan photo for AI. Reminders for your supplements and water are scheduled on the phone itself. Fitzio doesn’t use your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or Apple Health / Health Connect.

If your phone’s own backup (iCloud or Google) is turned on, it may include Fitzio’s data. That backup is in your own account and controlled by your phone’s settings.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run Fitzio, and only to provide the app:

  • Supabase: hosts our database and sign-in.
  • Anthropic: the AI service (Claude) that reads plans you choose to send. Anthropic processes this content to return the result and doesn’t use it to train its models.
  • Google and Apple: if you choose “Sign in with Google” or “Sign in with Apple”, they confirm who you are. They also deliver the app through their stores.
  • Google Fonts: the app loads its typefaces from Google’s font service. This is a normal web request; nothing you enter in Fitzio is sent.
  • Exercise videos: tapping a video link opens it in your browser on the video site (for example YouTube), under that site’s own privacy policy.

We disclose information to authorities only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Synced data stays for as long as your account exists. Items you delete are marked deleted so your other devices can catch up. When you delete your account, your sign-in and all synced data are removed from our systems; backups held by our hosting provider are overwritten within their normal backup cycle.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account: open the Account screen and tap Delete account (type DELETE to confirm). This removes your sign-in and everything synced to our database.
  • Delete a check-in, photo, workout, plan or supplement inside the app at any time.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your phone.

You can also ask us to delete your data, or for a copy of it, by email using the contact details below.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each person’s synced data is kept apart by access rules on the server, so an account can only read its own data.

Health information

Fitzio records the plan and progress you enter. It doesn’t give medical advice, diagnose anything, or change your plan for you. Talk to your coach, dietitian or doctor about your diet and training.

Children

Fitzio is meant for adults (18 and over) and isn’t directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above. For significant changes, we’ll also let you know in the app.

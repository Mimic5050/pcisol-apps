Common questions

How do staff or family members join my home?

On the owner’s phone, go to Settings and turn on sync. Settings shows a 6-letter join code. On the other phone, install Homezio, tap “I have a join code” and enter it.

How does Homezio know when something will run out?

It learns from your history. Each time an item is bought and later marked “Finished” — or bought again — Homezio works out how long it lasted. The more you use it, the more accurate the dates get. Before that, it uses the estimate you set for the item.

Can staff use it in their own language?

Yes. Tap the Language button on the staff screen and choose from 16 languages, including Sinhala, Tamil, Hindi, Filipino and Arabic. Item names are translated automatically, and the 🔊 button reads them aloud.

How do I scan a bill?

Go to Shop → Bill. Take a photo of a printed receipt, or add screenshots of an online order (up to 12). Check the items Homezio found, then save. Bill scanning needs an internet connection.

Does Homezio work offline?

Yes. Tapping items, the shopping list and forecasts all work without internet. Changes sync to the household’s other phones when you’re back online.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Delete account (or Erase data on a phone without an account) and type DELETE to confirm. See the privacy policy for exactly what is removed.
